Decision records

Six decisions behind Tutorial Rescue. Timings marked "dev" were measured in development on one video, not benchmarked.

ADR-1 · Vertex AI for everything, including YouTube video

Context
The API-key path hit prepaid quota limits, which made grounded checks fragile. Video ingestion had used the key client.
Decision
Run all reasoning on Vertex with service-account auth. Pass YouTube URLs as video parts with mime_type="video/*".
Evidence
"video/mp4" returns 500 INTERNAL on Vertex, verified 2026-10-18. Source: app/gemini.py, docs/architecture.md section 3.
Consequence
One backend, no key in the request path, postpaid billing. key_client() is just client() under USE_VERTEX=1. Rollback is one env var.

ADR-2 · Verify in parallel batches of 4, thinking low

Context
One grounded call for all steps was slow.
Decision
VERIFY_BATCH=4, batches run in parallel, VERIFY_THINKING="low". Batches keep the original step numbers so they merge without renumbering.
Evidence
On one 10-step video in development, verify went from about 108 s (one call, default thinking) to about 12 s. In the eval, the whole-tutorial verify median is 10.8 s, run one video at a time over 16 old tutorials. Accuracy at default thinking was not compared. Source: development log, 18 Oct 2026; eval.
Consequence
If one batch fails, its steps show unconfirmed and the result is not cached.

ADR-3 · Low media resolution for extraction

Context
Video tokens drive extraction time and cost.
Decision
VIDEO_RESOLUTION="low".
Evidence
Exact menu and button names were extracted from all 26 eval videos at low resolution. Extraction median was 21.6 s, one video at a time (holdout, 11.8–106.6 s), and slower under concurrent load (up to 651 s with 4 at once). Higher resolutions were not compared. Source: eval.
Consequence
Very small on-screen text may be misread. Not yet tested across many videos.

ADR-4 · STEP-block text, parsed by regex, not a second JSON pass

Context
Sources must be tied to the exact step they support.
Decision
The grounded call answers in a fixed STEP n: verdict / TODAY: ... format. A regex parses it into blocks with UTF-8 byte offsets.
Evidence
Grounding supports index the response's bytes, so offsets must point at the text we show. A second LLM pass would re-word it and break the link. A unit test covers byte offsets with Hindi. Source: app/rescue.py parse_blocks; tests/test_rescue.py.
Consequence
A step the model skips gets "not checked" and shows as unverifiable.

ADR-5 · The model never writes the public comment

Context
The comment is posted publicly under a creator's name.
Decision
update_card fills a template, English or Hindi, from sourced verdicts only. Unsourced steps appear only as "couldn't confirm".
Evidence
Unit tests: update card uses only sourced verdicts; empty without evidence. Source: tests/test_rescue.py; docs/architecture.md 3b.
Consequence
The wording is plain and repeatable. It is less flexible than free text.

ADR-6 · 7-day cache, never cache partial results

Context
A verdict is true "as of" its check date. Docs change, so old results go stale.
Decision
CACHE_DAYS=7. The result is stored only when no batch failed.
Evidence
Source: app/rescue.py analyze.
Consequence
A failed check can never be served as a good one. A repeat check within a week costs nothing extra.
