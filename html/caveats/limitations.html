Limitations

What Raahi does not claim, and where it can be wrong.

Tutorial Rescue

  • Verdicts are "as of" the check date. Results are cached for 7 days, then re-checked. app/rescue.py
  • Grounding can miss very recent UI changes. If Search finds nothing current, the step is marked unverifiable. It is not marked current.
  • "Official source" is a string match. It compares root domains with the list the model extracts for the tool. A vendor with several domains can be missed. rescue.mark_official
  • Transcript fallback is weaker in production. YouTube often blocks transcript requests from cloud IPs. Video ingestion is the main path. docs/architecture.md section 4
  • Cost. Every uncached check makes Vertex calls: one extraction plus one grounded call per 4 steps. We have no per-check cost figure in the sources. app/rescue.py
  • Extraction is slow under load. Median 21.6 s one video at a time, but up to 651 s with 4 videos at once. Demo videos are pre-warmed (7-day cache). eval/RESULTS.md
  • The eval is small and hand-reviewed by the builder. 16 cases, 10 controls. The run 2 prompt was tuned after seeing 6 of the controls; only the 4 holdout videos are an untuned precision check. Some "option not showing" cases may be account or region issues. eval results, eval/CASES.md

Competitor note

YouTube's own "Ask" feature on videos is the closest alternative we know of. Our understanding is that it answers questions about the video, but does not re-check each step against today's docs with cited sources. This is our understanding, not a verified claim.

From the README, "Honesty and limits"

  • Predefined vs AI: scenario cards are handwritten recipes, badged "predefined recipe". Typed goals go through the Gemini planner and are badged "ai-planned". Checks are live in both.
  • Verification: unknown check types are refused. Manual completion shows as "done (your word)", never verified. SPF/DMARC checks prove record presence, not inbox placement.
  • Studio: "silence" cuts are caption-gap candidates from transcript timing, not audio analysis. Cloud rendering is planned v2 and its button is disabled.
  • Security: outbound fetches refuse private and metadata addresses. The API is rate-limited per IP (demo-grade). There is no auth yet, so do not store secrets in profiles.
  • Not yet evidenced: completion-rate benchmarks, user study, willingness to pay. These are the v1 evaluation plan, not claims.
  • API-key path: grounding may exhaust prepaid quota; steps then arrive labeled unverified. Documented, not a pass.

Source: README.md.
